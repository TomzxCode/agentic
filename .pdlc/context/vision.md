# Product Vision

## Target Customer (ICP)

One developer who hands coding work to AI agents across many local repositories and manages it from a desktop or a phone.

## Vision

A developer who delegates to coding agents always knows what is in flight, what is waiting on someone, and how much of the work the agents finished alone, and can resume any task in under a minute.

## Positioning

For a solo developer working with coding agents, who struggles to keep task context scattered across threads, issues, and checkouts, Agentic is a local-first work tracker that keeps every task's context current and measures how much work agents complete without intervention.
Unlike hosted project trackers and agent dashboards, it runs on your machine against your own checkouts, drives any ACP-speaking harness, and records an audited change lifecycle instead of vibes.

## What Winning Looks Like (12 months)

Resuming any in-flight task takes under a minute because its summary and next steps are current.
The majority of accepted changes are touchless, and yield is measured and improving month over month instead of guessed.
The whole loop (triage, queue, review, park, resume) works from a phone.
Agents create and maintain most of the tracker's content through the CLI and scheduled runs.

## Non-Goals

- Teams, shared workspaces, or any multi-user future.
- Becoming a cloud service or syncing through someone else's server.
- Replacing git hosting, CI, or code review tooling.
- Two-way continuous sync with Slack, GitHub, or Drive.
